Yacht Engine Winterizing & Freeze Protection Guide

Freezing winter temperatures cause trapped raw water inside engine blocks, heat exchangers, and exhaust manifolds to expand and crack cast iron. Proper winterizing protects your vessel.

Raw Water Antifreeze Flushing

Non-toxic marine propylene glycol antifreeze is inducted through the raw water intake until colored fluid discharges freely from the exhaust.

Pre-Layup Engine Oil & Filter Change

Acidic combustion byproducts in old engine oil etch crankshaft bearings during winter dormancy. Fresh oil change before storage is essential.

Fuel Tank Stabilization & Biocide Treatment

Keep fuel tanks 95% full to minimize air space condensation and add high-grade marine biocides and fuel conditioners.

Domestic Fresh Water & Sanitation Drainage

Drain calorifiers (water heaters), pumps, deck showers, and heads to prevent ruptured plumbing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why replace closed-loop coolant before winter? Corrosion inhibitors degrade over time. Fresh coolant protects cylinder liners from cavitation and corrosion.
How should yacht batteries be maintained over winter? Maintain batteries on smart float charging or fully disconnect terminals after 100% state-of-charge.
How long does professional winterization take? Our mobile team completes full twin-engine, generator, and domestic winterizing in approximately 3-5 hours.
