Marine Generator Diagnostics & Raw Water Cooling Troubleshooting

Marine generators supply vital onboard power for air conditioning, galley equipment, and charging systems. Any disruption in sea water flow can shut down the unit within seconds.

Raw Water Pump & Impeller Replacement

The neoprene impeller is the most critical wear component. Running dry or ingesting silt tears vanes, which subsequently lodge into the heat exchanger tubes. Replace every 200 hours or annually.

Exhaust Mixing Elbow Clogging & Descaling

Carbon and salt scale accumulate inside the exhaust mixing elbow, restricting flow and creating dangerous backpressure that triggers thermal shutdowns under load.

Fuel Supply & Air Bleeding Issues

Micro air leaks in fuel suction lines or water contamination in the primary filter cause the generator to crank, run for a few minutes, and unexpectedly shut down.

Alternator & AVR Voltage Regulation

Fluctuations in 230V AC output or 50Hz frequency point to faulty Automatic Voltage Regulators (AVR) or rotating rectifier diodes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the generator not discharging raw water? Check that the seacock is open, sea strainer is clear of debris/jellyfish, and the pump impeller vanes are intact.
What causes Onan/Kohler generators to shut down with fault codes? Common culprits include low oil pressure, loss of coolant flow (high exhaust temp), or electrical overload.
How should generators be winterized? Flush the raw water circuit with fresh water, circulate non-toxic marine antifreeze, and remove the impeller for off-season storage.
